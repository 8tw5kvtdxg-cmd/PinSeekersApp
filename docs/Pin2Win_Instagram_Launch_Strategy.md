# Pin2Win Instagram Launch Strategy

**Prepared for business partner discussion**  
**September 2026**

## Executive Summary

Pin2Win should use Instagram to create local demand for an experience, not to explain software. The strongest message is simple:

> **Five shots. One target. How close can you get?**

Instagram should show the pressure, competition, reactions, near misses, leaderboard movement, and partner venues that make Pin2Win entertaining. The intended customer journey is:

**Instagram discovery → venue/location page → simulator booking → onsite QR scan → paid challenge entry → player result → new social content**

The first 30 days should focus on one repeatable local content system. Pin2Win publishes three Reels and one carousel each week, supports them with Stories during live activity, and collaborates with the partner venue at least once per week. The initial goal is not a large follower count. It is to prove that Instagram can produce venue interest, booking-page traffic, QR scans, paid entries, and reusable player content.

## Strategic Positioning

### What Instagram should sell

- The excitement of taking five pressure shots.
- The possibility of moving up a live local leaderboard.
- Friendly competition among golfers, friends, leagues, and venue staff.
- A fresh reason to book and revisit an indoor golf venue.
- Recognition for strong shots, personal bests, and verified winners.

### What Instagram should not become

- A feed dominated by software screenshots.
- A stream of repetitive prize advertisements.
- A technical explanation of payment or verification systems.
- A broad national golf page before the local model has been proven.
- Messaging that makes the experience appear to be wagering or a game of chance.

The preferred brand personality is competitive, energetic, credible, local, and welcoming. Pin2Win should feel like a golf challenge community supported by technology—not a technology company trying to manufacture a community.

## Audience Priorities

### Primary audience: local golfers

- Indoor golf and simulator customers.
- Recreational golfers who enjoy friendly competition.
- League players and golf groups.
- Golfers looking for social, date-night, or group activities.
- Players motivated by leaderboards and measurable improvement.

### Secondary audience: venue operators

- Indoor golf owners and managers.
- Golf bars and simulator lounges.
- Teaching studios, club fitters, and event operators.

Approximately 80% of the feed should speak to players and 20% should demonstrate partner value. This keeps the account entertaining while still giving prospective venues visible proof that Pin2Win creates activity and content.

## Profile and Account Setup

**Suggested profile name:** Pin2Win | Indoor Golf Challenges

**Suggested biography:**

> Five shots. One target.  
> Monthly indoor golf challenges.  
> Play at participating simulator venues.  
> Find a location below.

Create five Story Highlights:

1. **How It Works** — booking, onsite QR scan, payment, play, and verified result.
2. **Locations** — each participating venue and its booking link.
3. **Leaderboard** — current standings and weekly movement.
4. **Winners** — verified winners and player interviews.
5. **Rules** — eligibility, attempts, scoring, and challenge windows.

Pin these three posts at the top of the profile:

1. A 15-second “What is Pin2Win?” Reel.
2. A simple “How to play” carousel.
3. The current participating venue and active challenge.

General Instagram traffic should go to a public location or booking page—not directly to the protected onsite QR checkout.

## Content System

### Recommended content mix

| Content pillar | Share | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Challenge drama | 40% | Shots, near misses, reactions, pressure, and leaderboard changes. |
| Player stories | 25% | Introductions, personal bests, rivalries, and winner interviews. |
| Venue and community | 20% | Venue tours, staff attempts, group nights, and local atmosphere. |
| Education | 10% | How the challenge, entry, scoring, and verification work. |
| Company story | 5% | Building Pin2Win and launching new locations. |

### Sustainable weekly cadence

- **Monday:** Leaderboard or weekend recap Reel.
- **Wednesday:** Challenge, near-miss, or player Reel.
- **Friday:** Partner-venue collaboration Reel.
- **Sunday:** Educational or recap carousel.
- **Stories:** Live activity, polls, countdowns, reposts, and quick updates whenever players are active.

Each Reel should be vertical 9:16, use clear audio, keep essential text inside the safe zone, and communicate the premise immediately. Meta specifically recommends native 9:16 video with audio and safe-zone placement for Reels performance.

### Repeatable Reel concepts

1. **Near miss:** “This missed by less than two feet.”
2. **Pressure shot:** “His fifth and final shot changed the leaderboard.”
3. **Club choice:** “You have five shots. What club are you choosing?”
4. **New leader:** “We have a new leader—and it wasn’t close.”
5. **Staff challenge:** “The venue staff said they knew this simulator best.”
6. **Player profile:** “Meet the golfer everyone is trying to catch.”
7. **How it works:** “Book. Scan. Enter. Take five shots.”
8. **Monthly recap:** “The five closest shots from this month.”

Use one call to action per post: book the venue, save the post, tag a golf partner, or follow for the next leaderboard update.

## First 30-Day Campaign

### Week 1 — Introduce the experience

- Publish the core “Five shots. One target.” Reel.
- Publish the four-step “How Pin2Win works” carousel.
- Film the founder or a venue employee attempting the challenge.
- Publish a collaboration post announcing the venue.
- Build the five profile Highlights.

### Week 2 — Make it social

- Publish a slow-motion near miss.
- Introduce the current “golfer to beat.”
- Create a venue staff leaderboard.
- Run Story polls about club choice and predicted distance.
- Begin collecting permission to repost player content.

### Week 3 — Build competition

- Reveal weekly leaderboard movement.
- Publish “Can anyone take the lead?” content.
- Compare two strong shots side by side.
- Show the complete booking-to-QR-to-play journey.
- Add a countdown to the end of the challenge period.

### Week 4 — Create urgency and payoff

- Publish a final-week reminder.
- Release a best-reactions compilation.
- Announce and recognize the verified winner.
- Record a short winner interview.
- Tease the next monthly challenge.

### 30-day production target

- 12 Reels.
- 4 carousels.
- 4 partner collaboration posts.
- Stories on every active challenge day.
- At least 8 reusable player or staff clips.
- A measured baseline for booking clicks, QR scans, and paid entries.

## Partner Venue Activation

The venue is Pin2Win’s most important distribution partner. Each launch partner should be asked to provide:

- One Instagram collaboration post per week.
- Two or three Story reshares during active challenge weeks.
- Permission to film in appropriate simulator areas.
- One staff member willing to attempt the challenge on camera.
- A trackable venue booking link.
- Staff reminders that encourage onsite customers to scan the QR code.

Pin2Win should supply the venue with finished captions, edited vertical clips, Story graphics, leaderboard cards, and a simple posting calendar. Reducing the venue’s workload will improve consistency.

## Player-Generated Content Loop

Every verified player is a potential content contributor. After participation, invite the player to follow Pin2Win, tag Pin2Win and the venue, share a shot or reaction, and grant repost permission.

Do not offer cash or prizes specifically in exchange for likes, follows, shares, or comments. If a creator, player, or venue receives free play, payment, or another benefit for posting, use Instagram’s paid-partnership disclosure where required.

## Paid Promotion Plan

Paid promotion should begin only after approximately 15–20 organic posts reveal which concepts generate meaningful shares, profile visits, and booking clicks. Meta ads do not have a fixed price: ads are delivered through an auction, and actual costs vary with audience competition, location, objective, creative quality, placement, and season. The amounts below are planning budgets, not promised platform rates.

### Paid promotional opportunities

| Opportunity | Best use | Suggested test | What it provides |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Boost a proven Reel | Fast local awareness and social proof | $10–$15/day for 7 days ($70–$105) | The simplest way to extend the reach of an organic post, but with less control than Ads Manager. |
| Local traffic campaign | Core launch campaign | $20–$30/day for 14 days ($280–$420) | Sends nearby golfers to the public venue/location page and allows more control over objectives, audiences, placements, and measurement. |
| Warm-audience retargeting | Convert previous interest | $5–$10/day for 14 days ($70–$140) | Reaches prior video viewers, profile engagers, or tracked website visitors after the audience is large enough to use effectively. |
| Venue partnership ad | Borrow trust from the host venue | $150–$300 media test | Promotes an approved collaboration post from Pin2Win and the venue; both brands are visible. |
| Local creator partnership ad | Add authentic third-party credibility | $150–$300 media, plus negotiated creator compensation | Amplifies an approved creator Reel through a partnership ad. Free play or compensation requires the appropriate paid-partnership disclosure. |

Boosting a post is useful for a small awareness test. Meta Ads Manager is the recommended tool for the main campaign because it provides better control over the destination, geographic audience, placements, creative testing, and reporting.

### Recommended 30-day media budgets

| Pilot level | Media budget | Recommended use |
|---|---:|---|
| Lean validation | $300–$450 | Test two or three existing Reels near one venue and measure qualified traffic. |
| Recommended pilot | $600–$900 | Run a structured creative test, concentrate spending on the winner, and reserve a small amount for retargeting. |
| Accelerated local launch | $1,200–$1,800 | Test more creative, support multiple venue collaborations, and maintain meaningful reach throughout the month. |

The recommended pilot is **$600–$900 in media spend**, separate from content-production costs. If filming and editing are handled internally, production can remain low. If Pin2Win hires a local creator or editor, establish a separate project allowance; compensation can vary widely and should be negotiated based on deliverables and usage rights.

### Recommended campaign sequence

1. **Prepare:** Confirm Meta eligibility, connect the Instagram professional account and ad account, install available website measurement, create trackable links, and verify that the destination page clearly explains the offer.
2. **Test creative:** Run the near-miss/reaction Reel, the challenge explainer, and the venue-focused Reel against the same local audience. Change one major variable at a time so the result is interpretable.
3. **Concentrate spend:** After enough comparable delivery, pause obvious underperformers and direct most of the remaining budget to the creative producing the best combination of watch time, qualified clicks, and downstream entries.
4. **Retarget:** Once the audience is large enough, show deadline, leaderboard, or “book and play” messaging to prior video viewers, account engagers, and tracked site visitors.
5. **Review the full funnel:** Judge the campaign by location-page visits, venue-booking clicks, QR scans, and paid entries—not by reach or follower count alone.

### Audience and placement approach

- Start with adults located within a practical driving radius of the participating venue. Adjust the radius for local travel patterns rather than targeting an entire city by default.
- Begin broad within that geography; test golf or indoor-entertainment interests only as a separate audience instead of stacking many restrictions.
- Exclude staff and known internal testers where practical.
- Use Meta’s Advantage+ placements for the initial efficiency test, while supplying native 9:16 creative suitable for Reels and Stories. Meta recommends broad placement coverage and says Reels creative with 9:16 video, audio, and safe-zone placement performs better than image-only creative in its cited tests.
- Use partnership ads only when the venue or creator has approved promotion access and content rights are documented.

### How the cost should be evaluated

The most important internal number is the **maximum affordable customer-acquisition cost**, not a generic industry benchmark:

> Maximum affordable cost per paid entry = entry revenue − prize allocation − venue share − payment fees − other variable fulfillment costs − required profit contribution

For planning, Pin2Win can also model:

> Estimated cost per paid entry = cost per landing-page visit ÷ landing-page-visit-to-paid-entry rate

For example, if a landing-page visit costs $1.50 and 5% of those visitors ultimately become paid entrants, the modeled acquisition cost is $30 per entry. This is an illustration, not a forecast. Actual performance should be measured with tracked links, website events where permitted, venue booking data, QR scans, and payment records.

### Decision rules for the pilot

- Do not scale a post because it has inexpensive views if those views do not create qualified site traffic.
- Do not scale traffic solely because clicks are inexpensive if booking clicks, QR scans, and paid entries do not follow.
- Refresh creative when frequency rises and results deteriorate.
- Increase budgets gradually on proven ad sets; avoid frequent major changes that make results difficult to interpret.
- Stop or revise any campaign whose cost per paid entry exceeds the maximum affordable acquisition cost.

### Policy requirement before paid advertising

Meta states that accounts promoting online gambling, online real-money games of skill, or online lotteries require prior written permission. Because Pin2Win combines online paid entry with prize eligibility, the company should obtain platform-policy and legal confirmation before running paid Meta campaigns and confirm its broader account eligibility. Marketing must accurately present Pin2Win as an onsite golf skill and entertainment challenge and must not obscure the paid-entry or prize terms.

## Measurement Framework

### Content indicators

- Reel shares and saves per reach.
- Average watch time and completion rate.
- Profile visits generated by each post.
- Meaningful comments, direct messages, and player tags.

### Funnel indicators

- Location-page visits from Instagram.
- Venue booking-link clicks.
- QR scans by partner location.
- Paid entries divided by QR scans.
- Repeat entries and returning players.
- Reusable player clips collected.

### Paid indicators

- Cost per location-page visit.
- Cost per booking-link click.
- QR scans and entries associated with promoted periods.
- Paid-entry revenue compared with advertising expense.

Follower count should be treated as a supporting signal, not the primary measure of success. The key question is whether Instagram activity produces venue traffic, QR scans, and paid challenge entries.

## First 14 Days of Execution

1. Finalize profile name, biography, link, Highlights, and pinned-post plan.
2. Confirm filming and collaboration permissions with the launch venue.
3. Schedule one two-hour filming session with the founder, venue staff, and several consenting players.
4. Capture at least 20 vertical raw clips: setup, swings, ball flight, near misses, reactions, QR flow, venue exterior, and leaderboard views.
5. Edit the first six Reels before publishing the first one.
6. Prepare reusable leaderboard and winner templates.
7. Add tracking parameters to every venue booking link.
8. Record baseline Instagram, booking-click, QR-scan, and paid-entry data.
9. Launch the Week 1 content sequence.
10. Review performance after seven and fourteen days, then double down on the two strongest creative concepts.

## Decisions for the Partners

The business partners should agree on five items before launch:

1. Who owns weekly filming, editing, posting, and comment responses?
2. Which venue and geographic market will be the first Instagram focus?
3. What player and venue permissions are required before filming or reposting?
4. Which public page will receive Instagram traffic and track booking intent?
5. Who will confirm Meta advertising eligibility before any paid campaign begins?

## Recommendation

Approve a focused 30-day organic Instagram pilot around one active partner venue. Build the account around competition, personalities, and proof; make the venue a co-distributor; connect every post to the booking and onsite QR funnel; and delay paid advertising until Pin2Win has both proven creative and confirmed policy eligibility.
