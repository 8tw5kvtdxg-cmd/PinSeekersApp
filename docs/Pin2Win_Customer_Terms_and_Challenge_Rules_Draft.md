# Part III — Refund and Payment Issue Policy

## 1. Purpose

Pin2Win wants customers to receive the challenge entry they purchased. A customer is eligible for a refund review when payment was made but no usable entry or eligible attempt was provided, or when another qualifying failure described below occurred.

## 2. Qualifying Refund Reasons

A refund may be approved when records and submitted evidence establish that:

- Payment succeeded but no corresponding Pin2Win entry was created.
- The customer was charged more than once for the same intended entry.
- The required event code or challenge access was not delivered.
- A Pin2Win, venue, simulator, equipment, software, payment, or network failure prevented the attempt.
- The Challenge was cancelled, suspended, or closed before the customer could use the entry.
- The entry was completely unused and no eligible attempt was begun.
- The customer was charged an incorrect amount.
- Another documented payment or access failure prevented delivery of the purchased entry.

An entry may still qualify as unused even if an event code was displayed, provided Pin2Win can verify that no challenge attempt began and the code was not shared or misused.

## 3. Nonqualifying Reasons

A refund will generally not be issued merely because:

- The participant completed any portion of the eligible attempt.
- The participant was dissatisfied with the score or result.
- A result was rejected or the participant was disqualified for violating the rules.
- The participant supplied materially false or mismatched information.
- The participant shared, transferred, sold, or misused an entry or event code.
- The participant missed a separate venue reservation for reasons unrelated to Pin2Win or a qualifying failure.
- The request concerns a venue reservation charge not collected by Pin2Win.

Nothing in this section limits a refund or remedy required by law.

## 4. Deadline to Submit a Claim

A refund request should be submitted within 14 calendar days after the payment or qualifying failure, whichever occurs later. Pin2Win may consider a later request when the customer could not reasonably have discovered or reported the issue earlier.
