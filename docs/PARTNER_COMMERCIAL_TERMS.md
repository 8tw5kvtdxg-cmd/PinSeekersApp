# Partner commercial terms — decision pending

Updated September 18, 2026 following the founder’s clarification.

No partner compensation model has been agreed. A possible payment to the venue of **$3 or $4 per customer payment** is under consideration. This is a proposal, not a commitment, approved rate, signed agreement, or instruction to make payouts. No automatic partner payout is implemented.

Before quoting or signing, agree in writing:

- The selected dollar amount and which customer payments qualify: challenge entry only, every payment, or another explicit basis.
- Treatment of refunded, disputed, failed, duplicated, discounted, and complimentary entries.
- Venue attribution, reporting period, reconciliation, payment schedule, and responsibility for fees and taxes.
- Pilot dates, approved bays, onsite support, evidence duties, marketing permissions, termination, and dispute handling.
- Responsibility and documented provision for prize funding, claim review, challenge closure, and customer refunds.

At a $20 challenge entry, a $3 or $4 payment would leave $17 or $16 before processor fees, prize provision, support, marketing, refunds, taxes, and other costs. These are illustrative amounts only; the qualifying payment basis remains undecided.

Partner objectives remain more confirmed bookings, first observed visitors, and repeat visits. Report simulator bookings separately from challenge sales. A customer who books venue time without purchasing a challenge still creates partner value.
