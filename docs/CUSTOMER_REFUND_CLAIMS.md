# Customer-initiated refund review

September 19, 2026 — supersedes the automatic payment-alert workflow.

Automatic payment-issue detection, claim generation, internal alert generation, alert emails, and the scheduled checkout/entry scanning/recovery job are removed. Historical records remain read-only history; existing human-reviewed cases and refunds already requested remain available. Normal checkout completion and payment webhooks remain responsible for confirming payments and creating entries.

## Customer journey

1. In Account, choose **Request a refund review** (`/account/payment-issue`). The customer signs into their own verified account.
2. Select the payment and reason, describe what happened and why a refund is appropriate, and optionally supply the venue, incident date, evidence description and a screenshot/receipt. The account email identifies the customer. Existing server validation and ownership checks remain enforced.
3. Submit. The claim and submission event are saved together and immediately appear under **Admin → Refunds**. Submission does not guarantee approval.
4. Send an acknowledgement to the customer and a review email to staff, including the claim reference and submitted details. Staff follow the authenticated admin link to review attachments and decide.
5. Administrators can review, approve or deny with explanations. Issuing a Square refund remains a separate explicit administrator action after approval. Customer and staff receive decision/provider-status updates.

Staff recipients use the existing comma-separated `PIN2WIN_PAYMENT_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL` configuration; if unset, the recipient is `pin2wingolf@outlook.com`. No new third-party recipients or automatic venue recipients were added.

Delivery failures cannot discard the saved claim. Customer and staff deliveries are attempted independently, tracked separately and retried using stable idempotency keys. Immediate sending prioritizes the submitted claim instead of unrelated backlog. The existing scheduled endpoint is retained for pending claim-email retries, status updates for refunds already requested by administrators, and existing privacy/participation communications; it does not scan checkouts or entries for new problems. No new refund or claim can be generated by that scheduled job.

Tests cover acknowledgement/review contents, independent delivery failures and retries, suppression of old automatic notices, and the scheduled endpoint’s restricted responsibilities. No real customer email or provider refund is used in local tests.
