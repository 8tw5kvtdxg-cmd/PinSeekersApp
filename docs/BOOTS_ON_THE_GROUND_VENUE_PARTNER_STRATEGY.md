# Venue partnership working playbook

Commercial terms are undecided. Read [the current terms record](PARTNER_COMMERCIAL_TERMS.md) before outreach. The previous no-revenue-share playbook is retired.

1. Ask the venue which days and times need demand and which customers it wants to reach.
2. Confirm approved booking links and get permission for imagery, offers, signage, and customer stories.
3. Promote ordinary simulator bookings alongside the optional challenge. Keep booking handoffs functional even when challenge sales are closed.
4. Agree on a limited pilot budget and concrete marketing activities. Do not guarantee bookings, revenue, or prizes.
5. Reconcile venue reservations and confirm actual attendance. Ask how the customer heard about the venue and retain the supporting reference with consent and appropriate access.
6. Review completed bookings, reported referrals, first observed visitors, and repeat visits together. Do not represent clicks or QR page loads as customers or conversion rates.
7. Obtain written commercial terms and complete the supervised readiness rehearsal before opening paid challenges.
