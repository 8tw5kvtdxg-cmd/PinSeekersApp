# San Antonio-area indoor golf simulator prospect list

Last researched: September 16, 2026

## Scope and confidence

This is a living business-development list for Pin2Win. The core market includes San Antonio and nearby Bexar County communities; the extended drive market includes Boerne, Schertz, Castroville, La Vernia, New Braunfels, Canyon Lake, and San Marcos. Retail fitting studios are separated because they are not equivalent to entertainment venues. Businesses without a confirmed current address or opening status are kept on a watchlist rather than presented as operating.

The list was cross-checked against official venue websites, booking pages, municipal pages, and current simulator directories. No web search can prove that it found every new, unlisted, private, or recently closed business. Call or email before visiting. A venue's presence here does not establish simulator compatibility, permission to run Pin2Win, or compliance readiness.

## Exclusions and edge cases

- Topgolf San Antonio is a technology-enabled outdoor range, not an indoor screen-simulator business, so it is not in the primary list.
- Private country-club simulators, apartment/office amenity simulators, schools, and private home simulators are excluded unless they publicly sell access or memberships.
- Traditional outdoor ranges using TrackMan/Toptracer are excluded unless they also advertise enclosed indoor simulator bays.
- Alamo Golf Den and Alamo Golf Lounge appear to be distinct names and websites; verify ownership and operating status rather than assuming they are related.

## Suggested next verification pass

For every high-priority lead, confirm by phone: current operating status, owner/GM name, number of playable bays, simulator hardware and software version, E6 or tournament capability, ability to export shot/session/timestamp evidence, staffed versus unattended hours, permission for onsite QR signs, direct booking URL, and interest in Pin2Win marketing the venue. Record the answer and date in the companion CSV.
